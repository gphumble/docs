---
title: "Read email analytics"
description: "Compare campaign engagement and investigate delivery results without confusing opens with confirmed readership."
---

Open **Marketing → Email Analytics** for an overview of campaign activity. Open an individual campaign for its detailed results.

## Read the overview

1. Check that you are viewing the intended sending domain where a domain selector is available.
2. Review the campaign count, average open rate, average click rate, and engagement totals.
3. Locate the campaign in the table and compare its sent count, opens, clicks, and rates.
4. Open that campaign to investigate its individual results and delivery activity.

## Understand the measures

| Measure | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Sent | Messages submitted/processed for the campaign; not proof of inbox placement |
| Delivered | The receiving mail system accepted the message; not proof it was read |
| Total opens/clicks | Recorded events, which can include repeats |
| Unique openers/clickers | Distinct tracked recipients with the activity |
| Open/click rate | A percentage based on the report's measured activity and denominator |

Email activity is a signal, not a perfect measure of human behavior. Image blocking, privacy tools, and automated link checking can affect the recorded activity. Compare campaigns using consistent settings and consider replies or completed actions alongside tracking.

## Troubleshooting

If no analytics appear, confirm that a campaign has been sent and allow time for activity to arrive. Check tracking settings before expecting open/click data. If a campaign shows fewer sends than list members, review exclusions and suppressed recipients.

For one recipient who did not receive a message, use [Email troubleshooting](/email/troubleshooting). Do not treat a zero-open result as proof the recipient ignored the email.
