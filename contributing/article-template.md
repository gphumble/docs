## Before you begin

## Complete the task
Numbered steps, starting from a named page. Bold exact UI labels. Explain choices
where they occur; use a fictional example where it clarifies the decision.

## Check the result
What changes, where it appears, and what may take time. Distinguish saving a
record from charging a payment or delivering an email.

## Troubleshooting
Use symptom / what to check / recovery. Explain when to use Get support and
what non-sensitive information to include.

## Related tasks
