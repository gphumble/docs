When someone buys a ticket or wins an auction lot through a public event page but their email doesn't match an existing contact, the record lands in **Event match**, on the **Registrations** tab. Their ticket and confirmation already went out—this step links them to a contact and records the gift. The **Guests** tab is for the other people named on a registration.

<Note>
  Event match appears only when the Events module is enabled, and you need permission to manage event registrations.
</Note>

## What you'll see on Registrations

Each record shows when it happened, whether it's a **Ticket**, an **Auction win**, or a **New address**, the amount, the buyer's name and email, and the event (or lot). Filter by event if you have several.

Auction winners enter their name when they enroll to bid, so a win normally arrives with a name. A win with no name recorded shows only an email.

## Reconciling a record

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select a registration">
    Choose **Match** on a row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the contact">
    Choose a suggested candidate (matched on email, phone, or name). If nothing is suggested, or the suggestion is wrong, type at least two characters in the **search box** above the candidates to find the contact yourself by name, email, phone, or address. Otherwise **create a new contact** — buyer details pre-fill the new record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally save the address">
    If an address was captured at purchase, tick to save it to the contact. It becomes primary only if the contact has no primary address yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link & create gift">
    Choose **Link & create gift**. The gift is added to giving history and the registration is tied to the contact.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  **Skip this registration** marks it resolved without creating a gift, and can't be undone. The attendee keeps their ticket either way—skipping only affects your giving records.
</Warning>

## Match event guests

When one person registers several people, the additional attendees are guests. A guest whose email exactly matches one person in your records is linked automatically. Everyone else waits on the **Guests** tab, even when their name matches someone, because two people can share a name. Guests paid nothing, so matching one never creates a gift.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a guest">
    Open the **Guests** tab, filter by event if you need to, and choose **Match**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who they are">
    - **Add to** the buyer's household creates the person in that household. It's unavailable until the buyer is linked to a contact on the **Registrations** tab.
    - **Create as a new contact** creates a new household for them.
    - **Link to an existing person** lists likely people. Use the search box to find someone by name, email, phone, or address.
    - **Skip — not a real person** is for duplicates and placeholders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Correct the name if needed, add an optional note, and choose **Save match** (or **Skip guest**).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Check any person Advocora has already selected for you before saving. Once matched, the guest's attendance appears on their contact's activity timeline and counts in the **Attending** fields in [Queries](/queries/build-a-query). If the matched person is later deleted, the guest returns to this tab. A skipped guest does not.

Only guests on paid or comped registrations are listed. Refunded registrations are left out.

## Reviewing a new address

A buyer whose email matches a contact is linked automatically, so they don't need matching. But if they typed a street address at registration that the contact doesn't already have, the address comes here as a **New address** item so you can decide what to do with it. Nothing on the contact changes until you choose.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the item">
    Choose **Review** on a **New address** row. The panel shows the address given at registration next to the addresses already on the contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to do">
    - **Save and make primary** adds the address and makes it the contact's primary address. The previous primary stays on the contact as an additional address.
    - **Save as an additional address** adds it and leaves the current primary alone.
    - **Ignore this address** leaves the contact as it is.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An address counts as already on the contact when the street line and ZIP code match, ignoring capitalization, punctuation, and spellings such as "Trail" and "Trl". One item is created per order, however many tickets it covers. Ignoring an address doesn't remove it from the registration.

## Verify the reconciliation

After linking, open the retained contact and confirm the correct event/auction gift and amount. Compare the buyer details before accepting a suggested match. Matching does not sell another ticket or run another payment.

If a record remains in the queue after an error, check the contact and registration before retrying. If you cannot identify the buyer confidently, leave it for review rather than linking by name alone.
