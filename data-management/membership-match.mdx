An online membership sign-up is attached to a contact by email as soon as it's submitted, because payment needs one. When that attachment was uncertain, the sign-up is listed in **Membership match** so you can keep it or move it to the right person.

A sign-up from a new person with a new email isn't listed. There's nothing to check.

## Before you begin

Membership match appears under **Data Management** only when the Memberships module is enabled. You need permission to edit memberships.

Nothing you do here changes the payment, the card on file, or receipts.

## Why a sign-up is listed

| Reason | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| **Name differs** | The email belongs to someone in your records with a different name. It may be a shared inbox, or the wrong person. |
| **New household, name exists** | No contact had the email, so a new household was created, but someone in your records has the same name. |

Unpaid sign-ups are hidden. Select **Include unpaid (pending) sign-ups** to see them.

## Review a sign-up

1. Open **Data Management → Membership Match** and choose **Review** on a row.
2. Compare the submitted name, email, and address with **Currently attached to**. Open the contact or **Open membership** if you need more detail.
3. If the attachment is right, choose **Keep current contact**.
4. If it belongs to someone else, choose a person under **Move to**. To find someone who isn't listed, search by name, email, phone, or address.
5. Review the options:
   - Save the submitted mailing address to that contact. It never replaces an address they already have.
   - Move the email off the person it matched. Leave this ticked when the email belongs to the person you chose. Untick it for a shared household inbox, and the email is copied instead.
6. Choose **Move membership**. If the person isn't in your records, choose **Create new contact** instead; after you save the contact, the membership moves to it.

Choose **Skip** to close the row without changing the membership.

<Warning>
Moving a membership also moves its recurring gift and the gifts it produced. If the sign-up had created a new household and nothing else in Advocora refers to it, that household is removed. Each sign-up can be reviewed only once, so check the person before you confirm.
</Warning>

## Check the result

Open the contact you chose and confirm the membership and its gifts are there. Other people named on the membership stay on it.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A sign-up isn't listed | It may have been matched with confidence, already reviewed, or still unpaid. Select **Include unpaid (pending) sign-ups** and check the [member record](/memberships/manage-members). |
| **Someone else already reviewed this sign-up** | Reload the page and open the membership to see where it is now. |
| The chosen contact has no people | Add an individual to that contact, then review the sign-up again. |
| A membership was moved to the wrong person | Use **Get support** with the membership and contact names. |
