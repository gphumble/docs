---
title: "Find advocacy supporters"
description: "Filter synced supporters and open their linked CRM records."
---

Open **Advocacy → Supporters**. This list shows synced supporters, their engagement, activity, and link to an individual in Advocora.

## Find a supporter

1. Search by the available name/email search.
2. Narrow by **Tier**, **Status**, **Issue**, **Bill**, or **Action type** as needed.
3. Use the sortable column headings to compare name, engagement, action count, or last action.
4. Open the linked contact to review the relationship in the CRM.

The supporter status and advocacy engagement tier are different from the contact's giving-based donor status.

## Resolve a missing contact link

An unmatched or needs-review indicator means the supporter has not been confidently connected to a CRM individual. Follow it to [Match Review](/advocacy/match-review), inspect the evidence, and choose the correct person.

## Troubleshooting

Use **Clear filters** if expected supporters are missing. Check the dashboard's sync health if recent activity has not appeared. If the person is linked to the wrong household, correct the match rather than creating another supporter record.
