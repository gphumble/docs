---
title: "Match advocacy supporters to individuals"
description: "Review suggested links, create a contact when needed, and correct an incorrect match."
---

Open **Advocacy → Match Review**. The tabs are **Needs review**, **Unmatched**, **Skipped**, and **Linked**.

## Review a supporter

1. Search by name or email and open the relevant tab.
2. Compare the incoming supporter with the suggested individuals, including email and household information.
3. Choose **Link** for the correct candidate. If none is correct, use **Link to another individual — search by name or email**.
4. If the supporter is genuinely new, choose **Create new contact** and read the confirmation that a household contact will be created.
5. Use **Skip** when the record should not be linked or imported.
6. Confirm the resulting record is in the expected tab and inspect its contact link.

## Correct a link

Open **Linked**, find the supporter, and choose **Unlink** if the match is wrong. Read the confirmation, then review and link the appropriate individual. Use **Skipped** to revisit records you previously set aside.

## Troubleshooting

Do not accept a match on name alone when multiple people share that name. Open the candidates' contact records and compare other information. If no action is available or an action is denied, ask your Owner or Super Admin to review your access.

A match connects activity to a person; it is not a payment, gift, or permission to send marketing messages.
