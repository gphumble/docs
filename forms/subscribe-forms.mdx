---
title: "Create a subscribe form"
description: "Collect supporter details, record communication choices, and route signups to email lists."
---

Open **Admin Settings → Subscribe forms** to create a public signup form. It collects first name, last name, and email, with optional phone and address fields.

## Before you begin

Create the destination [email lists](/email/lists-and-segments). You need at least one selected list to publish the form. If you want a confirmation email, prepare a compatible template first.

## Build and publish

1. Create a form, enter its **Form name**, and keep **Status** as **Draft** while setting it up.
2. Under **Email lists**, select the lists this form should feed.
3. Under **Fields**, choose whether to collect phone and mailing address. Configure the email and phone/SMS opt-in checkboxes and their wording to reflect what the supporter is agreeing to.
4. Under **Confirmation email**, enable **Send a confirmation email after submission** if needed and select its required template.
5. Add branding, headings, thank-you message, disclaimer, logo, and background as needed.
6. Add allowed embed origins if the form will be used on another website.
7. Save, set **Published**, save again, and check the public link or [embed](/forms/embedding).

## What happens after submission

The submitted details enter the matching process. Review **Data Management → Subscriber Match** when a signup needs a person or household selected or created. Do not assume that a submitted email already has a completed contact record or final list membership.

If confirmation is enabled, check the template and recipient address when a message is missing. A submission can succeed even when a later email step needs attention.

## Troubleshooting

- **Cannot publish:** select at least one destination list and resolve any required-template message.
- **Expected field is missing:** check the field toggle and save the form.
- **Subscriber missing from a list/contact:** search for the email, then review [Subscriber match](/data-management/subscriber-match) before entering a duplicate.
- **Existing supporter remains unsubscribed:** inspect their communication preferences and suppression status; a new signup should not be treated as permission to bypass a prior opt-out without review.

Use a fictional test supporter and an address you control when checking the signup flow.
