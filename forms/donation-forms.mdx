---
title: "Create and publish a donation form"
description: "Configure amounts, recurring giving, receipts, gift routing, and a hosted or embedded donation page."
---

Donation forms accept contributions through your organization's connected payment account. Open **Admin Settings → Donation forms**, then create a form or open an existing one.

## Before you begin

You need donation-form management permission, a charges-enabled [payment account](/settings/payments), and a one-time receipt template. Recurring giving also requires a recurring receipt template. Prepare receipt content under [Email Templates](/email/templates).

## Configure the form

1. Under **Basics**, enter the **Form name**, choose the **Connect account**, and leave **Status** as **Draft** while preparing it.
2. Under **Amounts**, enter suggested dollar amounts separated by commas, such as `25, 50, 100, 250`. Set the default, minimum, and maximum amounts. Enable **Allow custom amount** if donors may choose their own amount.
3. Under **Recurring**, choose whether to enable recurring donations and select the intervals to offer.
4. Choose whether to show fee coverage and employer/occupation fields. Supply your organization's approved disclaimer or paid-for-by wording where applicable.
5. Review wallet-payment options. Availability to a donor also depends on their device/browser and the payment setup.
6. Set branding, logo, background, page heading, supporting text, and thank-you message. Review both hosted-page and embed appearance.
7. Under **Routing**, choose the gift designation, gift account, and optional project. A gift account categorizes the gift in Advocora; the Connect account processes the payment.
8. If using sub-projects, either pin the form to one sub-project or allow the donor to choose. Review the available choices and the donor-facing label.
9. Select the required receipt templates. Configure allowed website origins if embedding, then save.

## Publish and check

Set **Status** to **Published** and save. Copy the form's public link or [embed code](/forms/embedding). Open the public page while signed out and verify the amounts, frequency choices, wording, branding, and receipt setup.

An online payment is not the same as manually [recording a gift](/gifts/record-a-gift). Do not enter a second gift merely because a completed donation has not appeared in the contact record yet; check the payment and matching process first.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| No usable Connect account | Complete onboarding under Payments and refresh the account status. |
| Save requests a receipt template | Choose a one-time template and, when recurring is enabled, a recurring template. |
| Amount validation fails | Use positive dollar values, at least one suggested amount, and a minimum no greater than the maximum. |
| The public page is unavailable | Confirm Published was saved and use the copied public link. |
| A wallet button is missing | Check the form setting, browser/device support, and the embedded host's allowed origin. |
| A donor did not receive a receipt | Check the email address and receipt template. Preserve the payment details for support rather than asking the donor to pay again. |

See [Payments](/settings/payments), [Embedding forms](/forms/embedding), and [Recurring gifts](/gifts/recurring-gifts).
