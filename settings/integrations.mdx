The **Integrations** page connects Advocora to an outside email marketing provider, so you can keep using a tool your team already knows while your data stays in sync.

## Available integrations

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Mailchimp" icon="mailchimp">
    Sync email list members to Mailchimp audiences and pull engagement back.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Constant Contact" icon="envelope">
    Sync members to Constant Contact and pull open/click activity back.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Connecting

Choose **Connect** on a provider and follow the prompts to authorize it. Once connected, the provider shows a **Connected** badge and a **Manage** button for its settings.

<Note>
  You can have **one** provider connected at a time. To switch, open the connected provider's management page and disconnect it first—then connect the other.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Once connected, unmatched subscribers from your provider show up in [Subscriber match](/data-management/subscriber-match) so you can tie them to contacts.
</Tip>

## Conservative States

If the Conservative States integration is enabled for your organization, its own card appears on this page. It is separate from your email provider, so you can use both. Changing the connection or running a sync requires permission to manage integrations.

Advocora checks for new supporters and actions every few minutes while the connection is active. Use the **Sync** controls when you need more:

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Sync now** | Checks for new and updated supporters and actions right away |
| **Full re-sync** | Re-reads every supporter and action and refreshes every supporter profile. Existing links between supporters and contacts are kept. Large supporter lists take a while |
| **Refresh issue list** | Updates the list of issues |
| **Re-run matching** | Tries again to match supporters to contacts |
| **Pause** / **Resume** | Stops scheduled updates until you resume |

If the sync controls are unavailable, repair the connection at the top of the page first. See [Advocacy](/advocacy/overview) for working with the imported activity.

## After connecting

Open the provider's management page and review its list mapping and sync state. Check a sample individual and destination list before relying on a large sync. Use the corresponding unmatched-subscriber queue for people who could not be linked automatically.

If authorization fails, confirm you used the intended provider account and have access to its audience/list. If a sync fails, record the provider, affected list, time, and visible message for **Get support**. Advocora-native campaigns can be sent without connecting an external provider.
