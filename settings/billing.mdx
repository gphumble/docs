The **Billing** page shows your organization's usage charges: the email you send and, if AI is turned on for your organization, your AI usage. These charges are billed each cycle in addition to your subscription.

Open **Settings → Billing**. Only people with the **Owner**, **Super Admin**, or **Admin** role see this page.

## What you see

- **Current charges** — the running total for the cycle you are in. It is marked **Pending** and shows the date it will be billed. The total grows as you send email and use AI until the cycle ends.
- **Email** — campaign emails sent, receipts and other automatic emails, the number of billable emails, and the rate. Test and preview emails are listed but not charged.
- **AI usage** — the number of AI calls, tokens used (input and output), and images generated, with a breakdown by what the AI was used for. Calls that failed or that you stopped are listed but not charged.

## Billing cycles

Your billing cycle starts on the same day each month (your subscription anniversary) and ends the day before that date in the next month. Cycles begin and end at midnight in your organization's time zone. If a month is too short to have your billing day, the cycle starts on that month's last day.

If your billing day is ever changed, the change takes effect when the cycle in progress ends. Cycles that were already billed keep their dates, and the first cycle after the change runs from the old day to the new one, so it may be shorter or longer than a month.

## Look at a past cycle

Use the **Billing cycle** menu at the top of the page to choose an earlier cycle. The page then shows the charges and usage for that cycle and the date it was billed. Choose the entry that starts with **Current** to return to the cycle in progress.

## Questions about a charge

If a figure does not look right, note the billing cycle and the figure, then use [Get support](/getting-started/get-support). If the page shows an error instead of figures, choose **Try again**.
