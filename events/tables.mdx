A table tier lets one person take a whole table. That person becomes the table's **host**. They get a private page to manage the table and an invite page to share, and their guests register for seats from that invite page. Use tables for galas and dinners where supporters bring their own circle. Tables work for paid and free events.

## Before you begin

- You need permission to edit events.
- Turn on **Offer tables** for the event when you [create it](/events/create-an-event), or later by editing the event.
- A paid event needs its Stripe account ready before you publish.
- Decide who pays for the seats. You choose this on each table tier.

| Who pays | What the host pays | What a guest pays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The host pays for the whole table** | The table price | Nothing. Guests claim a seat at no charge. |
| **Each guest pays for their own seat** | The seat price for each seat the host fills themselves | The seat price for each seat they take |

Each payment is recorded as its own gift for the person who paid, so a guest's payment never reimburses the host. As with any ticket, a buyer who isn't recognized waits in [Event match](/data-management/event-match) before the gift is recorded, and a gift receipt is sent only when the event has a **Gift receipt template**. On a free event nobody pays.

## Add a table tier

1. Open **Events**, choose the event, then open **Ticketing**.
2. Select **Add tier**, or edit an existing tier.
3. Set **Type** to **Table**.
4. Enter **Seats per table** (2 to 50). Optionally enter **Tables available**, the number of tables that can be taken; leave it blank for no limit.
5. On a paid event, choose an option under **Who pays for the seats**, then enter the **Table price ($)** or the **Price per seat ($)**.
6. When guests pay, decide what happens to [seats nobody buys](#decide-what-happens-to-unsold-seats).
7. To give each guest a specific seat, not just a table, select **Designate seats**. You assign the seats later, when you [seat guests](/events/seating).
8. Select **Save tier**.

## Decide what happens to unsold seats

When each guest pays for their own seat, choose an option under **Seats still unsold when sales end** and set **Seat sales end**. The date must be before the event starts, in the event's timezone. Once it passes, guests can't buy seats at these tables and nobody new can take one.

| Option | What happens after seat sales end |
| --- | --- |
| **Leave them for staff to handle** | Nothing automatic. The end date is optional for this option. |
| **Release them to another ticket tier** | Each table shrinks to the seats taken and closes. The freed seats go on sale in the tier you choose. |
| **Charge the host for them** | The host's saved card is charged the seat price for each seat nobody bought. |

Releases and charges run automatically, usually within an hour or so of the end date, and only while the event is live and hasn't started. A table where a guest is in the middle of paying waits for the next run.

### Release them to another ticket tier

Under **Release unsold seats to**, choose a ticket tier that isn't a table, or select **Create a tier**. A tier created this way costs the price per seat and stays inactive until seats are released into it. On release, an inactive tier is switched on with only the released seats for sale, and an active tier with a limit grows by that number. An active tier with no limit isn't changed.

People who buy released seats are ordinary ticket buyers and don't belong to a host's table. A table with nobody left at it is cancelled.

### Charge the host for them

A host who isn't filling every seat must agree to the charge before taking the table.

- Checkout shows the price per seat, the date, the most seats they could be charged for, and the largest total. They must tick the box to continue.
- They pay for their own seats with a card, which is saved for the later charge. Bank payments and digital wallets aren't offered.
- If they chose to cover processing fees, the later charge includes fees too.
- The later charge stays within what they agreed to: never more seats, a higher seat price, or an earlier date, even if you change the tier or table. If you lower the seat price, they pay the lower price.
- The host isn't charged if every seat is taken, the table is cancelled, or their own registration was refunded or cancelled.

While seats are still unsold, the host is emailed 7 days and 1 day before the end date, and again when the card is charged. After the charge, those seats are the host's to give: guests can take them from the invite page at no charge.

<Warning>
  This is a saved card, not a hold on funds, so the charge can be declined. The open seats then stay closed and nothing is released. The host is emailed a link to update their card, and the table is flagged for your team. See [Manage table hosts and guests](/events/table-hosts-and-guests#settle-a-declined-charge).
</Warning>

## Check the result

1. [Publish the event](/events/publish-an-event) and open its public page. The tier shows **Table of** and its seat count, plus the tables left when there's a limit.
2. Choose the table. The buyer is asked for **Seats you are filling now**, counting themselves, and receives tickets for those seats only. The rest stay open for guests.

A table is taken on its own: choosing one replaces other tickets in the cart, and only one fits in an order.

## Troubleshooting

- **Table isn't in the Type menu:** turn on **Offer tables** for the event, then reopen the tier.
- **A table tier left the public page:** its **Seat sales end** date may have passed. Otherwise check that it's active, public, and within its sale window.
- **You changed a tier after tables were taken:** those tables keep their seat count, which you change on the **Tables** tab. Who pays, the seat price, and the end date apply to them right away, within the limits above. A host who never agreed to cover unsold seats isn't charged for them.
- **Invite pages stopped working:** they need a live event that hasn't ended, with **Offer tables** on and the tier still a table.
- **An older Table tier sells as a regular ticket:** **Who pays for the seats** isn't set. Choose an option to make it a hosted table.
- **You need to give a table away:** use **New table** on the **Tables** tab. **Add Registrant** doesn't accept table tiers.
