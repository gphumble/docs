Once people take tables, the event's **Tables** tab lists each one with its host, the seats filled, and anything that needs attention. This guide covers what hosts and guests see and what your team can do for them.

## Before you begin

- The event needs **Offer tables** turned on. See [Offer tables at an event](/events/tables).
- Viewing the tab needs permission to view events. Changing a table needs permission to edit events. Seating or moving a guest needs permission to manage registrations.

## What the host gets

A host's tickets arrive as usual. A separate email carries two links:

- **An invite page** to share. It matches the event's public page: logo, hero image or brand color, description, and venue map, plus the host's message when there is one. Advocora doesn't send invitations for the host.
- **A private page** to manage the table. No account is needed, so the host shouldn't forward this link. It works until a week after the event.

From the private page the host can:

- Set the **Table name** and write a **Message to your guests** (up to 1,000 characters).
- Choose **Show who is coming on the invite page**, which shows first names and last initials. When it's off, guests see only how many seats are open.
- See who has joined, and use **Close table** or **Reopen table**.
- Use **Add a guest yourself** while seats cost guests nothing. The ticket goes to the guest's email, or to the host without one.
- Replace the saved card, when they cover unsold seats and a charge could still be made.

The host is emailed when a guest joins and when joining guests fill the table.

## What a guest sees

A guest opens the invite page, chooses up to 10 seats, and enters their name, email, and mailing address. Free seats are saved at once. For paid seats the guest pays by card on the same page, and bank payments aren't offered. They can choose to cover processing fees. Tickets are emailed once the payment goes through, and the payment is recorded as the guest's own.

A seat being paid for is held for 30 minutes. A full or closed table shows as full, with a link to the event.

## Review your tables

1. Open **Events**, choose the event, then open **Tables**. The **Hosts and guests** view opens first.
2. Read the seats filled. A red count shows how many tables need attention.
3. Check each table's status. Only the most important one is shown.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Taking guests |
| **Full** | Every seat is taken or held |
| **Closed** | Closed to new guests by the host or your team |
| **Card declined** | The host's charge for unsold seats didn't go through. Needs attention |
| **Host withdrawn** | The host's own registration was refunded, cancelled, or removed. Needs attention |
| **Unsold seats charged** | The host paid for the seats nobody bought |
| **Charge waived** | Your team waived that charge |
| **Seats released** | Unsold seats were put on general sale |
| **Cancelled** | The table was cancelled |

4. Select **Open** on a row to work on that table.

## Help a host

- **Edit the table:** change **Table name**, **Host's message to guests**, or **Seats at this table**, then select **Save**. A table can't go below the seats already taken. One placed in the room can't outgrow its room table; move it to a bigger one first.
- **Change the host's contact details:** edit **Host name** or **Host email**, then select **Save**. A new email address is sent a new private link, and the old link stops working.
- **Hide a message:** select **Hide the host's message**. **Show the host's message** restores it.
- **Resend or replace links:** select **Email the host their links**. If the private link reached the wrong person, select **Send a new private link**; the old one stops working immediately.
- **Stop or restart sign-ups:** select **Close to new guests** or **Reopen to guests**. The host can't reopen a table your team closed.

## Seat or move a guest

- **Seat a guest at no charge:** enter a name and optional email, then select **Seat guest**. The ticket goes to the guest, or to the host without an email. This works even where guests normally pay. The seat counts as filled, so a host who covers unsold seats isn't charged for it.
- **Move a sign-up to another table:** select **Move** beside the guest, choose the table, and select **Move** again. Every seat on that sign-up moves, and what was paid stays with it. The move is refused if there isn't room. Seats on the host's own registration can't be moved.
- **Refund or remove a guest who signed up from the invite page:** use [Registrants](/events/registrants). Every seat on that sign-up reopens.

## Give a table away

1. Select **New table**.
2. Choose the **Table tier**. On a tier where guests pay, guests are still charged for their own seats, so choose one the host pays for if the whole table is free.
3. Enter the **Host name**, **Host email**, **Seats the host fills now**, and an optional **Table name**.
4. Select **Create table**.

The host pays nothing, is recorded as **Comped**, and is emailed their tickets and links. They aren't charged for unsold seats on any tier. The table counts toward **Tables available**.

## Release unsold seats yourself

When the tier's option is **Release them to another ticket tier**, your team can release a table's open seats before the end date.

<Warning>
  Releasing can't be undone. The table shrinks to the seats taken and closes, the host can't reopen it, and the freed seats go on general sale.
</Warning>

Open the table and select **Release unsold seats**. Seats the host has already been charged for can't be released. If a host still owed a charge, it's settled and won't be taken later.

## Settle a declined charge

The host can settle it from the link they're emailed: **Update card and pay** saves a new card and charges it straight away. Your team can:

- Select **Retry the card**. The seats still open are counted again, so seats filled since aren't charged.
- Select **Waive the charge**. The host isn't charged, and the open seats stay closed. You can also waive a charge before it's due; seats then stay on sale until the end date. A waived charge can't be put back.

The charge can be tried again only while the event is live and before it starts.

## Cancel a table

<Warning>
  Cancelling doesn't refund anyone. The host and guests keep their registrations until you refund them or move the guests.
</Warning>

Select **Cancel table**. The invite page stops working, the table and its guests leave the seating chart, and no charge for unsold seats is made while it's cancelled. **Restore table** brings it back, but you must place it in the room again.

## Check the result

Reopen the table to confirm its host, seats, and status. A notice that a link was emailed means it was queued, not that it was delivered.

## Troubleshooting

- **A host can't open their private page:** if the link was replaced, select **Email the host their links** to send the current one. Emailing it again doesn't extend a link that has expired.
- **The table is full but seats look open:** someone may be paying for a seat, which is held for 30 minutes.
- **A guest was charged, then refunded:** they paid after their seat was gone, for example by retrying a declined card. The payment is refunded in full automatically, with no ticket or gift. The guest is emailed an explanation, with a link to try again if there's room. The registration shows **Refunded**.
- **A guest can't buy a seat:** seats stop selling at the tier's **Seat sales end** date.
- **You named a new host, but the table still says Host withdrawn:** the status follows the original host's registration. The new host gets a private link, but nobody can be added by name; guests can still join from the invite page while it's open. Move the guests to another table if needed.

If a charge or refund looks wrong, use **Get support** with the event and table name. Don't include card details.
