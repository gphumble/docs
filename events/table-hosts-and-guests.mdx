Once people start taking tables, the event's **Tables** tab lists every table with its host, how many seats are filled, and anything that needs your attention. This guide covers what hosts and guests see, and what your team can do for them.

## Before you begin

- The event needs **Offer tables** turned on. See [Offer tables at an event](/events/tables).
- Viewing the Tables tab needs permission to view events. Changing a table needs permission to edit events. Seating or moving a guest needs permission to manage registrations.

## What the host gets

When someone takes a table they become its host and receive an email with two links:

- **An invite page** to share. The host is responsible for inviting their own guests. Advocora does not send invitations on the host's behalf. The invite page looks like the event's public page: your logo, the event's hero image (or brand color), your event description, and the venue map. The host's message appears as a note below your description, and is left out when there isn't one.
- **A private link to manage the table.** No account is needed. The link is the key, so the host should not forward it.

From their private page the host can:

- Name the table and write a message for the invite page.
- Choose whether the invite page shows who is coming, or only how many seats are still open.
- See who has joined, and close the table to new guests or reopen it.
- Add a guest by name on a table where guests do not pay, for someone who will not use the link.
- Replace the saved card, on a table where the host covers unsold seats.

The host is emailed when a guest joins and when the table is full.

## What a guest sees

A guest opens the host's invite page, chooses how many seats they need, and enters their details. If the seats are free they are saved at once. If guests pay for their own seats, the guest pays by card on the same page and gets their own receipt. Tickets arrive by email with a QR code for the door.

The page shows the table as full when every seat is taken or held. A seat a guest is in the middle of paying for is held for 30 minutes.

## Review your tables

1. Open **Events**, choose the event, then open **Tables**. The **Hosts and guests** view opens first.
2. Read the summary line for the number of tables and seats filled. A red count shows how many tables need attention.
3. Check each row's status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Taking guests. |
| **Full** | Every seat is taken. |
| **Closed** | The host or your team closed it to new guests. |
| **Card declined** | The host's card was declined for unsold seats. Needs attention. |
| **Host withdrawn** | The host's own registration was refunded or cancelled. Needs attention. |
| **Unsold seats charged** | The host was charged for the seats nobody bought. |
| **Charge waived** | Your team waived that charge. |
| **Seats released** | Unsold seats were put on general sale. |
| **Cancelled** | The table was cancelled. |

4. Select **Open** on a row to work on that table.

## Help a host

With the table open:

- **Rename the table or edit the host's message.** Change **Table name** or **Host's message to guests**, then select **Save**.
- **Hide a message.** Select **Hide the host's message** to remove it from the invite page without deleting it. Select **Show the host's message** to restore it.
- **Change the host's contact details.** Edit **Host name** or **Host email**, then select **Save**. When the email changes, a new private link is emailed to the new address and the old link stops working.
- **Resend the links.** Select **Email the host their links**.
- **Replace a link that went to the wrong person.** Select **Send a new private link**. The old link stops working immediately.
- **Change the table's size.** Enter a new number in **Seats at this table** and select **Save**. A table cannot be made smaller than the seats already taken. A table that is already placed in the room cannot be made bigger than the room table has space for; move it to a bigger room table first.
- **Stop or restart sign-ups.** Select **Close to new guests** or **Reopen to guests**. A table you close stays closed until you reopen it: the host cannot reopen it from their page.

## Seat or move a guest

- **Seat a guest at no charge.** Under **Seat a guest at no charge**, enter the guest's name and, if you have it, their email, then select **Seat guest**. With an email, the ticket goes to the guest. Without one, it goes to the host. This is a complimentary seat, including on a table where guests normally pay.
- **Move a guest to another table.** Select **Move** beside a guest who signed up from the invite page, choose the table, and select **Move**. What the guest paid stays with their registration. The move is refused if the other table does not have room.
- **Refund or remove a guest.** Use the [Registrants](/events/registrants) tab. Their seat reopens at the table.

Seats the host filled on their own registration cannot be moved one at a time. They stay with the host.

## Give a table away

1. Select **New table**.
2. Choose the **Table tier**. Pick a tier the host pays for if the whole table is complimentary. On a tier where guests pay, the guests would still be charged for their own seats.
3. Enter the **Host name** and **Host email**, and the number of **Seats the host fills now**.
4. Select **Create table**.

The host pays nothing, receives their tickets, and is emailed their invite and private links. The table counts toward the tier's **Tables available**.

## Settle a declined charge

On a table where the host covers unsold seats, a declined card closes the table's open seats until it is settled. Nothing is released automatically, because a declined card is usually fixed with a phone call.

The host can settle it themselves: the email they receive links to their private page, where **Update card and pay** saves a new card and charges it straight away.

From the Tables tab your team can:

- **Retry the card**, for example after the host confirms the card will now work.
- **Waive the charge.** The host is not charged. The open seats stay closed, because nobody paid for them.
- **Release unsold seats**, when the table's tier has a ticket tier to release seats to. The table shrinks to the seats taken and closes, and the freed seats go on general sale. The host is no longer charged for them, and cannot reopen the table. This cannot be undone. Seats a host has already been charged for cannot be released.

## Cancel a table

Select **Cancel table**. The invite page stops working, and the table and its guests are removed from the seating chart. Guests keep their registrations until you move or refund them, because they may have paid. Select **Restore table** to bring a cancelled table back.

## Troubleshooting

**A host cannot open their private page.** If you sent a new private link, older links no longer work. Ask the host to use the most recent email, or send another.

**A guest says the table is full but seats look open.** Another guest may be paying for a seat. A seat is held for 30 minutes during checkout and is released if the payment is not completed.

**A guest was charged and then refunded.** If a guest pays after their seat is no longer available, for example by retrying a declined card after someone else took the last seat, the payment is refunded in full automatically and no ticket is issued. The guest is emailed to say what was refunded and why, with a link to take a seat again if one has opened. The registration shows as **Refunded** with a note.

**A guest cannot buy a seat.** On a table where guests pay, seats stop selling at the tier's seat sales end date.

**You cannot seat a guest.** A table whose host was refunded has no active registration to add guests to. Enter a new host's name and email, move the guests to another table, or cancel the table.
