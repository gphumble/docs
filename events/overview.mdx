The **Events** module runs your fundraising occasions end to end—from a draft to a published registration page, through ticket sales and RSVPs, to a final tally of what you raised and spent.

<Note>
  Events is an optional module. If you don't see **Events** in the sidebar, it isn't enabled for your organization or your role doesn't include access. An administrator can turn it on.
</Note>

## The events library

Open **Events** in the sidebar to see your library, grouped by where each event is in its life. Each card shows quick metrics—**Sold**, **Gross**, **Expenses**, and **Net**. These are *collected* figures: ticket sales, paid sponsorships, auction lots whose winner was charged, and other completed gifts on the event, minus expenses marked paid. Incomplete checkouts are never counted. When unpaid sponsorships, uncharged auction bids, or unpaid expenses would change the picture, the card also shows **Proj.** — the projected net over everything entered, paid or not.

### Event statuses

| State | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Draft | Prepare settings and ticketing before publishing. |
| Live | Review the public registration page and incoming sales. |
| Closed / past | Review attendance, expenses, and reconciliation; public sales are no longer open. |

Use the available actions on the event for transitions. Completed events retain their records.

## How an event comes together

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a draft">
    Name it, set the date and venue, and choose free or paid. See [Create an event](/events/create-an-event).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up ticketing">
    Add ticket tiers (and merch, if you sell it). See [Ticketing](/events/ticketing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Polish the public page">
    Add a hero image, description, and colors. See [Public page & emails](/events/public-page-and-emails).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Pass the pre-flight checks and go live. See [Publish an event](/events/publish-an-event).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the day">
    Track [registrants](/events/registrants), check people in, and manage [add-ons](/events/add-ons).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Inside an event

Open any event to find tabs for **Overview**, **Ticketing**, **Registrants**, **Emails**, and **Public page**—plus optional tabs for **Sponsors**, **Expenses**, **Auction**, and **Volunteers** when you turn those on. The Overview tab gives you a KPI strip, a short "story so far," and a recommended next step. The **Net** tile uses the same collected math as the card, with the projected net shown beneath it when the two differ. The **Sold** tile counts tickets; when tiers admit more than one person per ticket it also shows the number of **admissions**, which is the head count to plan for. If guests on someone else's ticket aren't matched to a person yet, select **Match guests** to resolve them in [Event match](/data-management/event-match).
