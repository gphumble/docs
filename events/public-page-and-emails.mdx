Two tabs control what attendees see and receive: **Public page** and **Emails**.

## The public page

On the **Public page** tab, shape the registration page buyers land on:

- **Subtitle** and **Description** (rich text, shown as "About this event")
- **Brand color** and **Accent color**
- A **hero image** (PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 8 MB)—or enable **Allow color-block hero** to use a solid color instead
- Toggles to **show sponsors** and an **auction preview** (when those add-ons are on)

<Warning>
  You need a hero image **or** the color-block option enabled before the event can [publish](/events/publish-an-event).
</Warning>

## Embedding on your website

You can embed the event two ways: a **list** of your upcoming events, or a **single event** checkout. Both drop into your site as an auto-resizing snippet.

### Allowed embed origins

Use the **Allowed embed origins** list to control which websites may embed the event. Leave it empty to allow any site; add your domains to lock it down.

<Note>
  If a site that isn't on the list tries to embed the event, visitors see an "Origin not allowed" message. Add the domain here to fix it.
</Note>

## Attendee emails

The **Emails** tab controls the three automatic messages. For each you can edit the **Subject**, **Heading** (the large title at the top of the email), **Message**, and **Reply-to**, and send yourself a preview. Merge tags work in the heading; leave it blank to use the default wording shown in the field:

| Message | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Confirmation | Registration/ticket information after tickets are issued |
| Reminder | Pre-event attendee reminder, when enabled |
| Thank-you | Post-event follow-up, when enabled. Ends with one follow-up line: a link to the auction while any lot is still taking bids; otherwise thanks to the bidders (if the event had an auction) and a link to a published donation form, or a general request for support if you have none |

A ticket [reassigned](/events/registrants) to someone else is left out of the buyer's emails; its new holder gets their own when they have an email address.

Save the settings after editing. Ticket codes and links are supplied by the event email system; check the preview before sending tests.

<Tip>
  Send yourself a test of each email before you publish, so you know exactly what attendees will receive.
</Tip>
